An asset-disposal notice can look like a transaction story. For a key-account salesperson, it is more useful as a customer-change signal. The verified source describes a proposed sale of an old Chongqing plant by Lummy Pharmaceutical. The asset list includes six buildings, one solid-dosage production line, seven structures and one industrial land parcel. Production-line equipment is also to be moved to the Chayuan formulation plant. The notice does not disclose counterparty, amount or date. That silence is part of the lesson: separate public fact from sales inference, then use the PSS5 five capabilities to decide what to ask next.

## Start with Public Facts, Not Sales Assumptions
The source supports a limited set of facts. The sales task is to see what they may imply without turning implication into fact. Use the table as a boundary.
| Public fact in the source | What it does not prove |
| --- | --- |
| Proposed sale of the old Chongqing plant | That a buyer, price or timetable has been settled |
| Six buildings, one solid-dosage line, seven structures and one industrial land parcel | Which assets will be sold, leased, retained or demolished |
| Equipment to move to the Chayuan formulation plant | That relocation timing, capacity or responsibility are already resolved |
From there, read the operational changes: which assets are exiting, which capabilities are migrating, and which roles may participate. The source gives enough detail to frame those questions. It does not give enough detail to answer them for the customer.

## Value: Translate the Asset List into Business Outcomes
Value capability asks the salesperson to turn product features into the customer's profit, investment and operating value. Do not lead with equipment or engineering services. Ask what operating result the customer wants to protect: reducing idle assets, restoring output after the line move, or completing a compliant transfer. Prepare a value description with inputs, benefits and recovery logic, but do not invent a return on investment when the amount is missing. List unknowns such as the effect of the equipment move on output, the holding cost of idle assets, transaction taxes and timing. Map the six buildings, seven structures and land parcel to use, lease, transfer or demolition options, then ask the customer to rank objectives. Put the line move and Chayuan receiving capacity on one timeline. Separate the operations manager's use needs from the finance and approval layer's recovery requirements.
### A Value Conversation Starter
- Which operating result must this disposal protect: lower idle-asset burden, faster production recovery, compliant transfer, or another result?
- Do the buildings, structures and land parcel share one objective, or does each need a different path?
- Which interface between the line move and the Chayuan plant is most likely to stop output?
- What evidence would let the customer say the disposal achieved its purpose?

## Relationship: Map the Decision Chain Without Inventing Roles
Relationship capability combines professional insight, decision information and verifiable trust. The source does not disclose the transaction counterparty or an internal owner, so do not assume that a department has decided. Use PRTABCD to separate known from unknown: P for past or historical information, R for needs, T for time and key nodes, A for Arm or the role embracing the change, B for budget, C for competitors and alternatives, and D for the Decision chain. A asks who in the organization is embracing the change; that person may sit in asset management, production operations or finance. D asks about a multi-person, multi-department decision chain rather than one final approver. Keep an evidence table with source and next question. A blank unknown is better than a filled guess.

## Questions: Turn the Notice into Testable SPIN Questions
Questioning capability uses situation, problem, implication and need-payoff questions. The asset list and relocation destination are legitimate situation-question entry points, but they should not become an interrogation. Prepare questions and let the customer fill the answers:
- Situation: Who currently uses and maintains each building, structure and the land parcel? What is the receiving arrangement at Chayuan?
- Problem: Which link in relocation, sale, compliance or handover is most likely to stall?
- Implication: If disposal and relocation move at different speeds, what carrying costs or handover risks appear?
- Need-payoff: Which result should be solved first, and what evidence would show success?
Do not write sales assumptions as customer facts. If the customer declines to answer, record the unknown.

## Process: Use a Two-View Funnel to Avoid Jumping Ahead
System capability uses a two-view funnel: sales stages and customer purchasing stages. The sales stages are contact, needs, solution, negotiation and closing. The customer stages are problem recognition, value judgment, confirming a solution exists, responsibility, build or buy, solution and supplier selection, and commitment. The two views are many-to-many, not a mechanical one-to-one map. The public notice shows a proposed sale and relocation; it does not prove that the customer has reached supplier selection. A quote at that point would be premature. Contact is not a sent brochure. Record progress when the customer agrees to a next business conversation, shares background beyond the public list, or refers a relevant role. Use customer behavior evidence, not self-feeling.

## Negotiation: Prepare Alternatives Before Conditions
Negotiation capability rests on power, time, information and alternatives. With no disclosed counterparty or amount, do not assume price room. If later discussions occur, define BATNA, the best alternative to a negotiated agreement, for both customer and salesperson. Clarify bottom lines, authority, interests and records. Tradeable conditions can include scope, time, payment, risk sharing and acceptance criteria. The customer may have different department rhythms for asset sale and line relocation. Sales can discuss which segment comes first, who owns interfaces, and which assumptions belong in a memo. When amount and date are undisclosed, prepare a condition list rather than a number.

## Turn the Signal into Tomorrow's Actions
Four actions follow from the source. First, keep a customer change-signal ledger for asset sales, relocations and line adjustments. Second, use PRTABCD to mark known and unknown. Third, prepare SPIN questions for the next conversation. Fourth, use the two-view funnel to check whether you are advancing ahead of the customer. A one-page scan can record the public action, existing evidence, who to ask next and the next customer behavior to watch. A manager can review that page for evidence quality rather than visit count. PSS5 moves the conversation from 'I think there is an opportunity' to 'what the customer has done and what evidence is still missing.'

## Actionable Checklist
- Record only supported facts: proposed sale, six buildings, one line, seven structures, one land parcel and equipment movement to Chayuan.
- Mark counterparty, amount and date as unknown unless the customer supplies them.
- Map each asset class to use, lease, transfer or demolition, then ask for the customer's objective ranking.
- Put line relocation and Chayuan receiving capacity on the same timeline and identify the stop-risk point.
- Prepare a value narrative with inputs, benefits and recovery logic, but no invented return figure.
- Build a PRTABCD table; leave unknown cells blank and cite the source of each entry.
- Draft SPIN questions for situation, problem, implication and need-payoff; do not treat assumptions as answers.
- Locate the customer purchasing stage and your sales stage separately.
- Define progress by customer behavior: next meeting, new background, referral or a completed decision task.
- Prepare BATNA and a condition list before discussing price or schedule.

## FAQ
### What is the most common judgment error with this kind of notice?
Treating an asset-disposal announcement as a purchase order, or filling in counterparty, amount and date that the source does not provide. The notice supports a proposed sale and an asset list. Everything else should be a question or an evidence gap.

### Why should key-account sales pay attention to asset disposal?
Asset disposal can signal that a customer is changing how assets are used. It may involve relocation, handover, compliance, idle-asset recovery and operating continuity. The sales use is to identify possible issues early and verify them through conversation, not to assume a procurement event.

### How does value capability begin here?
Translate the asset list into business outcomes the customer may want to protect. Ask whether the old-plant sale is mainly about reducing idle assets, supporting the line move or completing a compliant transfer. Ask whether the buildings, structures and land parcel have the same objective. Shape the solution's value language only after those answers.

### How should relationship capability handle missing role information?
Use PRTABCD to separate known from unknown. Do not assign an internal owner from the outside. A asks who is embracing the change; D asks about the multi-person decision chain. Record the source and next question for every entry. Blank cells are acceptable; invented roles are not.

### Why does the two-view funnel matter?
It prevents the salesperson from mistaking activity for customer progress. The public notice does not prove the customer has reached supplier selection. Use customer behavior evidence to decide whether to offer information, a solution or conditions.

### What should negotiation preparation include when information is thin?
Prepare BATNA, bottom lines, authority, interests and records. Build a tradeable list covering scope, time, payment, risk sharing and acceptance criteria. Do not guess price room when amount and date are undisclosed. A condition list is appropriate preparation.
