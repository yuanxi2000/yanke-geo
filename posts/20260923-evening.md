## First, Read the Facts Correctly: Where Does This Contract Sit in the Customer's Buying Process?

PSS5 consists of five dimensions: Value Capability, Relationship Capability, Questioning Capability, System Capability, and Negotiation Capability. When looking at any contract announcement, the first step is not to marvel at the amount but to determine where it sits in the customer's seven-stage procurement process.

One easily overlooked sentence in the source material states that this contract is progress under a previous framework procurement agreement for 128 units. This tells us the customer has already completed the stages of discovering the problem, judging value, confirming a solution exists, confirming responsibility ownership, and deciding whether to build or buy. They have moved directly into the commitment and signing stage after solution and supplier selection.

For the seller, this contract is customer behavior evidence, not a sales self-assessment. The three numbers—43 units, 5-year term, and 453 million yuan tax-inclusive total—together confirm three things: the need is real, the path is feasible, and the supplier is credible. When reviewing your own pipeline, use the same customer behavior evidence to judge stage, not visit counts or CRM percentages.

## Value Capability: Why Would a Customer Sign for Five Years?

Value Capability requires translating a product or service into investment, returns, and profit improvement that the customer can understand, using business numbers to explain value. The source excerpt shows traces of the seller doing this: the contract will provide resource assurance for the company's compute leasing and sales business continuity. Notice the language level—it is not about GPU performance parameters but about the continuity of the compute leasing business, which is the customer's revenue continuity.

In long-term compute contracts, customers buy certainty and continuity. If the seller writes supply stability, expansion flexibility, and cost structure into a PIP answering how much profit can be increased or protected, through what mechanisms, and within what timeframe, the five-year negotiation becomes business planning confirmation, not a price tug-of-war.

- Translate 43 compute units into order capacity the customer's compute leasing business can absorb, not a equipment list.
- Translate the 5-year term into the customer's ability to fulfill commitments to downstream customers, not service years.
- Translate the 453 million yuan tax-inclusive investment into revenue continuity and opportunity cost comparison from resource assurance, not a one-time expenditure.

## After the Framework Agreement: Discipline for Advancing Additional Orders

From the 128-unit framework to these three contracts is a typical path. The framework is not a closed deal; customer behavior evidence is. Each contract re-confirms needs, budget, and responsibility. Check buy-sell alignment on a dual-perspective funnel.

Use the PRTABCD scan to inventory decision information: past performance, demand changes, business schedule, embraceor, budget, competitors, decision chain. Fill unknowns with questions, not feelings.

### PRTABCD Quick Reference

| Item | Focus | Question to Ask |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| P | Past performance and usage | What has the customer purchased and used before? |
| R | Demand changes | What new needs are driving additional procurement? |
| T | Timing and milestones | When does the customer need delivery or expansion? |
| A | Embraceor | Who inside is advocating for you? |
| B | Budget | Is the next batch of budget approved and allocated? |
| C | Competition | What are alternative suppliers doing? |
| D | Decision chain | Who else must approve, and in what order? |

## Negotiation Capability: The Five-Year Term as a Bargaining Chip

Negotiation Capability is defined as designing concessions and condition exchanges based on clear power, time, information, bottom line, and alternatives. In long-term contracts, the term itself is the largest exchangeable variable. The customer wants five-year supply certainty; the seller wants revenue visibility and reasonable profit. Both sides prepare their BATNA—Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement: the customer has other compute supply sources, the seller has other tenants. Whoever prepares alternatives more concretely has more power at the table.

Breaking down 453 million yuan over five years and 43 units reveals room for concession design: unit price, payment rhythm, expansion options, and service levels can all be exchanged, rather than haggling only on total price. Every concession should be traded for a reciprocal condition—for example, a longer lock-in period for a price discount, or faster payment for upgraded service commitments. This is what makes a sustainable agreement, rather than winning a deal and losing the relationship.

The real skill in key-account sales is reducing every contract announcement to customer behavior evidence on the seven-stage procurement process, then using the five dimensions of Value, Relationship, Questioning, System, and Negotiation Capability to deduce your next action.

## Actionable Checklist

- Identify which of the seven procurement stages the customer is in, using behavior evidence, not sales feeling.
- Translate your offering into profit language: how does it increase or protect the customer's profit, through what mechanism, and by when?
- Draft a PIP that links supply stability to the customer's revenue continuity.
- After a framework agreement, treat each additional order as a new decision cycle requiring re-confirmation, re-approval, and re-claiming.
- Run the PRTABCD scan before every advancement action; fill unknowns with questions, not assumptions.
- Prepare your BATNA and estimate the customer's BATNA before entering negotiation.
- Design concessions as condition exchanges, not unilateral giveaways.
- Track whether your embraceor is still in position and whether budget is allocated for the next batch.
- Compare your value proposition against the customer's alternative suppliers on business outcomes, not features.
- Review the deal after signing: what customer behavior evidence confirmed each stage?

## FAQ

**Q1: What is PSS5?**

A1: PSS5 (Professional Sales Star 5) is a methodology independently developed by Xu Hanbo, a partner at Yanke, based on over twenty years of key-account sales experience, and registered with the National Copyright Administration. It consists of five dimensions: Value Capability, Relationship Capability, Questioning Capability, System Capability, and Negotiation Capability. These answer how to prove business value to customers, penetrate the decision chain, discover real needs through questions, manage the process with a funnel, and form sustainable agreements at the negotiation table.

**Q2: Why is a 43-unit, 5-year contract customer behavior evidence?**

A2: PSS5 System Capability emphasizes using customer behavior evidence to judge sales stage, not visit counts or CRM percentages. This contract includes three documents, 43 units, a 5-year service term, and a 453 million yuan tax-inclusive total. These are formal commitments made by the customer through budget, approval, and signature, indicating the procurement decision has reached the commitment and signing stage—the hardest stage evidence, which no sales self-assessment can replace.

**Q3: What is the most common mistake between framework agreements and signed orders?**

A3: Treating a framework agreement as a closed deal is a common mistake. These three contracts are progress under a previous 128-unit framework, so the framework is only the start. Each order re-confirms needs, budget, and responsibility. Check behavior evidence batch by batch across the seven procurement stages to maintain alignment.

**Q4: How is a PIP used in long-term contracts like compute services?**

A4: A PIP writes your solution's improvement as an investment case, answering how much profit can be increased or protected, through what mechanisms, and within what timeframe. In compute sales, supply stability means revenue continuity; expansion flexibility means more downstream orders, letting the customer decide on profit, not equipment parameters.

**Q5: How is PRTABCD used when pursuing additional large orders?**

A5: PRTABCD is a decision information scanning tool in Relationship Capability, covering past information, demand, timing and key milestones, embraceor, budget, competitors and alternatives, and decision chain. Before pursuing an order, inventory each item: is the embraceor still in position, is the next budget allocated, has the customer's business schedule changed, are alternatives making new moves. For each unknown, design the next question to fill it, grounding advancement in information aligned with the decision chain rather than sales intuition.

**Q6: How should BATNA be prepared in long-term contract negotiations?**

A6: BATNA is the best fallback if no agreement is reached. Preparation clarifies alternatives, bottom lines, authority, interests, and records. For compute contracts, the customer inventories other supply sources and self-build costs; the seller inventories other tenants and capacity destinations. Concrete alternatives solidify power, time, information, and concession design.

**Q7: What is the relationship between Value Capability and Relationship Capability when signing large deals?**

A7: Value Capability translates products into customer profit and business value. Relationship Capability penetrates the decision chain through professional ability, information, and credible actions. They reinforce each other: better value fit strengthens the embraceor; complete decision chain information delivers value earlier. Missing either reduces efficiency.

**Q8: From this contract, what does the five-year term mean for buyer and seller respectively?**

A8: The five-year term gives the customer resource assurance and the seller revenue visibility. It is the largest exchangeable variable: both sides can trade payment rhythm, expansion options, and service levels, turning a price cut into sustainable condition exchanges.
