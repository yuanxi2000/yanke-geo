## Relationship Capability Reads Decision-Chain Information

PSS5 relationship capability is defined as building customer relationships through professional ability, decision information, and trustworthy action. Its focus is understanding and covering the decision chain, not equating relationship with entertainment. A board resolution is decision-chain evidence: it shows the customer has a mechanism requiring board-level votes on resource allocation. It also suggests information around the matter will concentrate upward.

Use PRTABCD: record knowns, unknowns, source, next questions. Check time/decision chain; other cells await talks.

## Map the Customer's Stage Before You Advance Your Stage

PSS5 system capability uses two axes: the customer purchasing seven stages and the sales five stages. Customer side: problem discovery, value judgment, confirming a solution exists, confirming responsibility ownership, build versus buy, solution and vendor selection, and commitment and signing. Sales side: contact, needs, solution, negotiation, closing. The axes are many-to-many and do not require mechanical matching.

A board approval for outbound investment to build a production base may sit in responsibility ownership or in build versus buy. The announcement cannot distinguish. Write both as hypotheses and eliminate them with customer behavior evidence. Do not decide the customer's stage internally and then reverse-engineer actions from that guess.

Misalignment: sales rush to solution/quote before owner, responsibility, or budget source is confirmed. Watch customer behavior.

## Turn the Resolution Into Questions, Not Conclusions

Use MBVS to generate hypotheses about one key person through four lenses: motivation, benchmark, vision, and solution. All four are hypotheses to verify. Do not turn a job title or inference into a customer fact. For this signal:

- Motivation: why is the customer pushing production-base investment now—capacity, delivery, cost, or compliance pressure?
- Benchmark: who in the same industry has done something similar, and how does the customer judge that approach and its results?
- Vision: after completion, what business changes does the customer want, and which metrics would look different?
- Solution: if an external supplier participates, which part of cost, efficiency, or risk could it influence?

Questioning can use SPIN according to the PSS5 questioning terminology, but the four question types need not be expanded here. The sequence need not be rigid, but every question should have intent and update a row in PRTABCD. Negotiation capability is largely unusable now. There is no quote, no confirmed counterparty, and no clear deadline. Discussing concessions is premature. BATNA and similar negotiation terms are not defined here; according to the PSS5 negotiation terminology, that preparation belongs before entering negotiation, not when information is thin.

## What to Do at the Lead Stage

The greatest value of a public resolution is that, before contacting the customer, you know a board-level event has just occurred. At this stage, do three things:

- Write knowns and unknowns separately.
- Prepare one specific question for each unknown.
- Find a way to confirm who inside the company is driving the matter.

Do not write a proposal or quote at this step. A proposal comes after the customer has confirmed the problem is worth solving and someone has claimed responsibility. A quote comes after scope and acceptance criteria are clear. Lead-stage work is lead-stage work. This is process discipline, not excessive caution.

## Store the Signal in the Right Capability

Many key-account sales problems are not a lack of information. They are a lack of a place to put information once it arrives. PSS5 offers five locations, each answering a different question:

- Relationship: who is driving this, who needs information, who can veto.
- Questioning: why the customer is doing this now.
- Value: what this means for the customer's profit and operations.
- System: which stage the customer is in and what behavior evidence exists.
- Negotiation: if negotiation begins, what to exchange and what alternatives exist.

The Wuhan Tianyuan announcement mainly falls into relationship and system. The other three lack actionable information for now. Leave them until dialogue begins and details can be filled in gradually.

## Actionable Checklist

- Record only verifiable facts: board meeting, three proposals, and the limited categories they cover.
- Mark empty fields: counterparty, amount, timeline, owner, budget, and external purchasing scope.
- Classify the signal as a decision-chain trace, not purchase intent.
- Fill only PRTABCD cells with evidence; leave the rest blank until a conversation.
- Write two customer-stage hypotheses: responsibility ownership and build versus buy.
- Prepare MBVS questions for one key person, not a generic list.
- Identify behavior evidence to watch: internal owner named, acceptance criteria discussed, budget language used.
- Do not draft a solution or quote before problem confirmation and owner assignment.
- Look for the internal driver through legitimate professional channels and public information.
- Update notes after every real customer conversation.
- Keep uncertainty visible so the team does not treat assumptions as facts.
