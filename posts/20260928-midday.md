A joint vehicle launch between two large organizations is easy to read as industry news and move past. For key-account sales teams, it is more useful as a structural case study. When two major entities choose to bind their names together in a single release, they are demonstrating how value bundling, relationship management, and alternative-option design work at scale.

This guide relies on one verified source: an East Money Information report on the Qijing GX7 launch, a vehicle from GAC Group and Huawei Qiankun cooperation. The source confirms three facts: the cooperating parties are GAC Group and Huawei Qiankun under the Qijing brand; the event is the GX7's formal market launch; and the product covers three versions: Ultra, Ultra dual-motor AWD, and Ultra tri-motor AWD. Price, order value, and launch date were not given. Sales analysis should respect that boundary.

## Why This Matters to B2B Sellers

The reason to study this event carefully is that your own key accounts are doing something similar. They are looking for strong partners, fitting their capabilities into someone else's value chain, and then facing the end market under a joint name. If you can read how they choose partners, how they bind together, and how they layer a product line, you can see what role you should be playing inside your customer's organization.

## Value Capability: Translating Ability into Business Language

PSS5 is a five-capability framework for key-account sales, built from value capability, relationship capability, questioning capability, system capability, and negotiation capability. The first of these is value capability, defined as shifting the sales conversation away from product features and toward the investment, return, and profit improvement a customer can understand, expressed in business numbers.

The GAC and Huawei Qiankun cooperation is, at its core, a two-way proof of value capability.

Apply that test to your own business. When a customer executive asks what they get from buying from you, and your answer is a feature list plus a discount, you are still speaking at the product layer. Convert the proposal into revenue growth, cost reduction, or risk avoidance, and retell it in language a customer CFO would recognize. That is the first step of value capability.

## Relationship Capability and the PRTABCD Scan

Two groups reaching the point of a joint launch must have passed through a long period of inter-organizational adjustment. Relationship capability in PSS5 is defined as building customer relationships through professional ability, decision information, and credible action, with coverage focused on the customer's decision chain rather than treating relationships as entertainment.

At the operational level, the PRTABCD scan is a way to inventory the intelligence you hold:

- P (past): Who did the customer choose for the last comparable cooperation, why, and with what result?
- R (requirement): What problem is the customer actually solving this time, and who defined it?
- T (timing): Where are the customer's key milestones, a launch window or a budget window?
- A (advocate): Who inside the customer organization is embracing you and willing to speak for you?
- B (budget): Which budget line applies, and who holds approval authority?
- C (competition): Who else is on the customer's list of alternatives, and what are the comparison criteria?
- D (decision chain): Across a multi-person, multi-department chain, what is each link's position and influence?

Of these seven categories, A and D are the most commonly neglected.

## Questioning Capability: Three Versions as a Set of Customer Questions

Launching the GX7 with three versions at once is not a simple product lineup. It is a layered answer to market demand. Seen through questioning capability, the product team must have answered a series of questions before launch: which customer segment cares about range and performance, which cares about the entry price threshold, and which is willing to pay for the top configuration. These questions connect to the four-part SPIN question structure: establish the situation, locate the difficulty, amplify the impact, and finally lead the customer to state the need themselves.

You can design questions the same way. Instead of opening with a product demonstration, ask how many times the equipment stops each month, which production line a stoppage affects, and whose performance review is hit when that line delivers late. When the customer calculates the impact themselves, the reason to buy is no longer something you stated. It is something they accepted.

## System Capability: Judging Progress with a Dual-View Funnel

System capability in PSS5 manages the process through a dual-view funnel made of five sales stages and seven customer purchasing stages. The five sales stages are contact, requirement, proposal, negotiation, and closing. The seven purchasing stages run from problem discovery through to commitment and signing. The market launch of the Qijing GX7 corresponds to activity after the full chain has been walked. Every project in your own pipeline may be sitting at some step along those seven stages.

A practical method is to mark two positions for each active project: which of the seven purchasing stages the customer has reached, and which of the five sales stages your own actions have reached.

## Negotiation Capability: Strong Partners Change the Table

Negotiation capability is defined as designing concessions and condition exchanges on the basis of clarity about power, time, information, bottom line, and alternatives.

For sellers, the implication is to answer three questions honestly before negotiating a large deal. If this deal does not sign, what is my best alternative? What is the customer's best alternative? Can I create a new option for the customer that another supplier cannot offer? When you can lay the weaknesses of the alternatives on the table for the customer, price negotiation becomes value negotiation. Preparing a BATNA should cover five concrete items: an executable alternative, a bottom line, authority limits, interests, and records. It cannot remain a verbal notion.

## Five Actionable Steps

1. Write the profit improvement your cooperation can bring onto a single page, framed as an investment case rather than a quotation.
2. This week, fill the gaps in your PRTABCD intelligence, focusing on the advocate and decision chain.
3. Mark each active project against both the seven purchasing stages and the five sales stages, and identify misalignments.
4. Before your next visit, design your question order around situation, difficulty, impact, and return on need. Talk less, ask more.
5. Before negotiating, write out both your own and the customer's best alternatives, then decide the order of concessions and the conditions to exchange.

Key-account selling is less about who speaks better and more about who sees the structure earlier: where the value sits, how the decision chain runs, and what the alternatives are worth. GAC Group and Huawei Qiankun demonstrated this kind of structured binding through a single joint launch, and the same thinking can be applied inside your own customer organization using the five dimensions of PSS5.

## FAQ

### Q1: What does the public information actually confirm about the Qijing GX7 launch?

Three points are confirmed: the cooperating parties are GAC Group and Huawei Qiankun under the Qijing automotive brand; the event is the formal market launch of the GX7; and the product covers three versions, Ultra, Ultra dual-motor all-wheel drive, and Ultra tri-motor all-wheel drive. Price, order value, and the specific launch date were not given in the available information. Business analysis should hold that boundary and not invent numbers.

### Q2: What can B2B key-account sellers learn from a joint vehicle launch?

Three things. First, when large customers choose partners, they look at whether the other side fills a capability they cannot build themselves or cannot build quickly. Second, a relationship that reaches a joint announcement rests on decision information and credible action, not entertainment. Third, a layered product launch implies systematic demand questioning and market segmentation beforehand. These map to value capability, relationship capability, and questioning capability in PSS5, and they transfer directly to your account management.

### Q3: What are the five dimensions of PSS5?

PSS5 is a five-star capability framework for professional key-account sales. Value capability translates a product or service into customer profit, investment, and business value. Relationship capability penetrates the customer's decision chain through professional insight, decision information, and verifiable trust. Questioning capability discovers and amplifies real needs through deliberate question design. System capability manages the sales process with funnels, stage judgment, and genuine progress. Negotiation capability builds sustainable agreements around power, time, information, and alternatives.

### Q4: How is the PRTABCD scan used in practice?

PRTABCD uses seven categories of decision information to expose blind spots: past history, requirement, timing and key milestones, advocate, budget, competition and alternatives, and decision chain. The method is to record, item by item, what is known, what is unknown, the source of the information, and the next question to ask. It belongs to relationship capability as a decision-information scanning tool rather than a persona profile. Each completed item should lead to who to ask next and what to ask.

### Q5: What is a dual-view funnel, and why is it better than a single funnel?

A dual-view funnel places the seven customer purchasing stages and the five sales stages on one chart.

### Q6: How should a BATNA be prepared in key-account negotiation?

BATNA is the best alternative to a negotiated agreement, meaning the best fallback action available if the parties cannot agree.

### Q7: How is SPIN questioning applied in a key-account visit?

SPIN advances through four question types.

### Q8: How do you judge whether progress in the funnel is real?

The standard is customer behavioral evidence, not visit counts, CRM percentages, or the seller's feeling.
