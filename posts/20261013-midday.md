## Why a Capital-Use Change Matters

A public filing about changing how raised funds are used is easy to overread. It does not confirm a customer is shopping or reveal who decides or how procurement will run. It gives you concrete material for the next visit: a written commitment you can use to prepare sharper questions rather than inventing conclusions. When a customer moves money from one project to another, they are ranking priorities with capital. That does not signal demand for your product; it shows a capability moved forward and another back. The spending channel is open for something. The manager layer using the money must deliver a business result to the capital-allocation and approval layer. If your proposal connects to that intended use, the conversation is an investment case. If not, you are seeking budget outside a stated purpose.

## The Five Dimensions Ask Different Questions

The framework referenced is PSS5, Yanke's five-capability framework for key-account sales, used in sales training, capability assessment, and practical coaching. It is a product and framework, not a company and not generic PSS. Its dimensions are value, relationship, questioning, system, and negotiation capability. Applying PSS5 to one announcement produces distinct inquiries: What business result does this money buy, and can my proposal be an investment case? Who can verify this, who embraces it, who is missing? What are the situation, problem, implication, and need-payoff, and what is my next question? Where is the customer in purchasing, and has my action run ahead? What leverage, timing, information, and alternatives do I hold? Reading through five lenses is more useful than concluding once.

### What Value Capability Translates

Value capability converts the sales object from features into an investment, return, and profit improvement expressed in business numbers. Box 1 is the capital-allocation and approval layer; Box 2 is the manager layer operating the funds. Help Box 2 argue for Box 1's money using business value. PIP, the profit improvement plan, writes the proposal's operational improvement as an investment case: how much profit is added or preserved, through what mechanism, in what time frame. It is not a quotation. Monetization first identifies which operating step the proposal affects, quantifies improvement, then converts it into revenue, cost, profit, payback period, or opportunity cost.

## Relationship Capability: The Announcement Is Only an Entry Point

Relationship capability builds relationships through professional ability, decision information, and credible action, emphasizing coverage of the customer's decision chain. PRTABCD uses seven categories to find intelligence blind spots: past information, needs, time and milestones, embrace person, budget, competitors and alternatives, and decision chain. Each records what is known, unknown, the source, and the next question. The A is Arm, the embrace person. The D is Decision, a chain involving multiple people and departments. MBVS takes a single key person and generates and verifies hypotheses through four lenses: motive, benchmark, vision, and solution. These are hypotheses, not customer facts. One announcement fills only a few cells; visits complete the rest.

## Questioning Capability: Ask Where Money Is Already Spent

SPIN advances needs discovery through situation, problem, implication, and need-payoff questions, moving from latent dissatisfaction to explicit needs. Begin with what businesses the Wuhan operations center will take on and what daily operations the office space and computing power support. Then ask where conditions are stuck, what results suffer if the blockage continues, and what return resolution brings. Do not skip the order. The customer should answer. A salesperson who concludes for the customer shrinks the need.

## System Capability: Judge the Stage Before Acting

The seven-stage purchasing process describes decision tasks from discovering a problem to committing to a contract, each requiring customer behavioral evidence. Shengtian Network's action sits on the build side: buying an office building, adaptive renovation, and putting office space and computing power into company-owned assets. The next judgment is which steps outside that path are more economical to supply externally, comparing capability, time, and total cost. The five-stage sales process and the seven-stage customer purchasing process form a dual-view funnel. If the customer still evaluates a build option and you rush a quote, your action has run ahead, making later steps passive.

## Negotiation Capability: Put Your Chips on the Table Early

Negotiation capability designs concessions and conditional exchanges based on clear leverage, time, information, bottom line, and alternatives, pursuing a sustainable agreement. BATNA is the best alternative to a negotiated agreement: the best fallback when parties cannot agree. Prepare executable alternatives, bottom lines, authority, interests, and records. With a customer that has written capital use into an announcement, your room for concession depends more on information quality and alternatives. Write those down before discussing conditional exchanges.

## Prepare Three Things Before the Next Contact

- **Evidence table.** Write the seven PRTABCD categories as a table, marking knowns, unknowns, sources, and next questions. One announcement will not fill it; blanks are for the next visit.
- **Investment case.** Following PIP, state which operating step is affected, how improvement is quantified, and the revenue, cost, profit, payback period, or opportunity cost. Give it to the manager layer for the approval layer.
- **Stage map.** Mark your position once with the five-stage sales process and once with the seven-stage purchasing process, writing behavioral evidence beside each. Do not substitute visit counts for progress.

## Three Common Pitfalls

- Treating a public announcement as demand confirmation.
- Treating available funds as your opportunity, ignoring the assigned purpose.
- Entering only after comparison and selection, leaving little negotiating room.

## Actionable Checklist

- Draft a one-page PRTABCD evidence table before any visit.
- Write one question per SPIN type, in order.
- Identify which operating step your proposal touches and its financial result.
- Convert it into a PIP-style investment case with a time frame.
- Mark customer and your stage separately with behavioral evidence.
- List unknowns about embrace person, budget, competitors, and decision chain.
- Prepare BATNA, bottom line, authority, interests, and records before concessions.
- Distinguish verified facts from announcement assumptions.

## FAQ

**What can be directly read from this announcement?**

Verifiable facts are limited. Shengtian Network announced changing remaining raised funds from cloud gaming to a Wuhan comprehensive operations center. It mentions purchasing an office building, adaptive renovation, company-owned office space, and computing power needs. This indicates money toward foundational capabilities; it does not confirm intent to buy your product, responsibility, or procurement.

**Why is a capital-use change a value signal?**

The money's purpose shifted, signaling altered priorities. Which business is pushed back and which capability built earlier is in the flow of funds. The manager using money must deliver results to the approval layer. If your proposal matches intended use, it's an investment case; if not, find budget elsewhere.

**With incomplete intelligence, how do you proceed?**

Use PRTABCD to list knowns, unknowns, sources, and next questions. Announcements fill few cells; visits verify embrace person, decision chain, competitors, and alternatives. Turn one unknown into known next contact.

**How is value capability used, and PIP versus monetization?**

Monetization identifies the affected operating step, quantifies improvement, and converts it to financial terms. PIP writes improvements as an investment case. For Shengtian Network, understand Wuhan operations businesses and computing power needs, then link to a financial result.

**The customer builds its own space and computing power. Is there opportunity?**

Public information shows build-side action, indicating investment in controllable assets and foundational capabilities. Judge which steps outside that path are more economical externally, comparing capability, time, and total cost. The seven-stage process places this at build-or-buy, where risk and switching cost matter. Verifiable evidence is needed to show external options are more stable.

**What should be prepared for negotiation?**

Clarify leverage, time pressure, information, bottom line, and alternatives. BATNA is the best fallback. Specify executable alternatives, bottom line, authority, interests, and records. Design concessions only after writing these down.

**Common errors reading this public information?**

Three: treating the announcement as demand confirmation; treating available funds as your opportunity, ignoring assigned purpose; and entering late after comparison and selection. A steadier approach treats it as preparation, uses PRTABCD to list unknowns, and brings an investment case to the manager layer.
