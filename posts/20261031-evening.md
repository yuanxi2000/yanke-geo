## Use the five capabilities together

PSS5 treats account judgment as connected work. Value capability translates business problems into investment and profit questions. Relationship capability identifies people who influence purchasing. Questioning capability turns gaps into answerable questions. Process capability compares sales activity with customer purchasing tasks. Negotiation capability helps prevent premature commitments.

Use all five when assessing an event. A plausible value hypothesis still needs decision participants, discovery questions, evidence of purchasing progress, and boundaries for commitments. A contact's interest alone cannot supply those missing elements.

## Map relationships with PRTABCD

PRTABCD provides seven categories for an account scan. For each category, capture what is known, what remains unknown, the information source, and the next question.

- **Past:** What relevant history is established?
- **Requirement:** What requirement has the customer acknowledged?
- **Time:** What timing is confirmed, and what remains unknown?
- **Arm:** Who within the customer organization supports working with your team?
- **Budget:** What evidence exists about funding or the budget process?
- **Competitor:** What competing options are known?
- **Decision:** How do people and departments participate in the decision?

### Distinguish a supporter from the decision chain

Arm identifies an internal supporter. Decision describes how the wider organization reaches a decision. One person may provide useful access without representing everyone who must evaluate, fund, approve, or implement a purchase.

For the Sunwoda Electronic event, ask who is driving the matter, who is checking its scope, and who would be accountable for the result. Treat possible participants as unconfirmed until evidence establishes their roles. Assign an owner to each unresolved question.

## Turn missing information into discovery questions

SPIN structures questioning around Situation, Problem, Implication, and Need-payoff. Use it to clarify the customer's decision tasks without implying that the public report has already established a purchase.

A practical sequence is:

1. **Situation:** What does the customer formally recognize about the event, and which details require confirmation?
2. **Problem:** Does the customer acknowledge a business gap connected with it?
3. **Implication:** If that gap exists, what consequences does the customer recognize?
4. **Need-payoff:** What improvement would make addressing the gap worthwhile?

Then establish who accepts responsibility and whether the organization is considering internal development or external purchasing. These questions follow the source's progression from an acknowledged gap to ownership and sourcing choices.

Keep questions within the available factual boundary. An unknown amount should lead to a verification task, not a question that presumes a particular investment. An unknown date should remain unresolved until an appropriate source confirms it.

## Compare sales activity with customer purchasing progress

The dual-view funnel places seller activity alongside customer decision tasks. The two views answer different questions and should be assessed together.

| View | Stages | Basis for assessing progress |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales | Contact, needs discovery, proposal, negotiation, closing | Seller activity checked against customer behavior |
| Customer purchasing | Recognize the problem, assess value, confirm a solution exists, assign responsibility, choose internal build or external purchase, select solutions and suppliers, commit and sign | Evidence that the customer has completed the relevant decision tasks |

Do not force a one-to-one correspondence between the two stage lists. Instead, ask which customer task the current sales action helps complete.

Evidence might include a customer review meeting, agreed evaluation criteria, a confirmed budget process, or explicit ownership of the problem. Without customer behavior supporting the judgment, the Sunwoda Electronic report does not establish that an opportunity has reached proposal or negotiation.

Visit counts and seller confidence are insufficient grounds for advancing a forecast.

## Express value through business variables

Value capability translates products or services into investment, returns, and potential profit improvement. When information is incomplete, begin with the structure of a financial hypothesis and leave unsupported figures unfilled.

List the variables that need validation:

- Costs the customer may need to reduce.
- Revenue the customer may need to protect or improve.
- Risks the customer may need to address.
- Cycle times the customer may need to shorten.
- Investment and payback considerations.

For each variable, ask what evidence would establish its relevance and who can confirm it. Useful questions include which profit the customer wants to protect, which losses it wants to reduce, and which role owns the result.

A working profit-and-loss hypothesis organizes discovery. It does not justify adding an unverified amount, return, or payback period to a proposal. Validate figures with customer data or formal documents before treating them as commitments.

## Prepare negotiation boundaries before concessions

Negotiation capability considers power, time, information, and alternatives when preparing a sustainable agreement. In an incomplete opportunity, clarify those conditions before discussing concessions.

BATNA means the best alternative to a negotiated agreement: the best available action if agreement cannot be reached. Make that alternative executable. The source suggests considering other customers, other solutions, or different uses of internal resources when an opportunity cannot be verified.

Record the alternative action, minimum acceptable conditions, decision authority, interests, and negotiation history. Revisit these as information develops.

For this event, the missing counterparty, amount, and date remain reasons to verify scope before making commitments. Quoting too early can turn uncertain assumptions into obligations. Conceding too early can allow missing evidence to shape negotiation terms before the underlying opportunity is understood.

## FAQ

### Which details can be used immediately?

The supplied material establishes the named organization, Sunwoda Electronic, and identifies technode as the public source. It does not establish the counterparty, amount, or date. Preserve those distinctions in the account record.

### Why does a headline not establish purchasing intent?

A headline indicates that a public report exists. Purchasing progress requires evidence of customer action, such as a review, an acknowledged requirement, a budget process, or responsibility being accepted.

### Can one contact represent the whole decision?

Do not assume so. PRTABCD distinguishes an internal supporter from the decision chain. Establish how the relevant people and departments participate instead of treating one contact's position as organizational agreement.

### How can a team discuss value without numbers?

Identify the relevant cost, revenue, risk, investment, and payback variables. Mark their values as unverified and seek customer data or formal documentation. That creates a business discussion without inventing returns.

### What should happen if the opportunity remains unverified?

Keep unresolved fields open, avoid unsupported stage advancement, and use the documented alternative action to guide resource decisions. Further activity should address a specific evidence gap or customer decision task.
