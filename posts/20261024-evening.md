Treat public leadership changes as external signals: record the fact, separate assumptions, and verify whether information entry points, decision chains, approval rhythms, and evaluation criteria actually moved.

## Separate the Public Fact From the Sales Judgment

According to the public source Eastmoney, the CPC Central Committee decided that Wang Zhijun would serve as a member and Standing Committee member of the Hunan Provincial Party Committee and as Secretary of the Hunan Provincial Party Committee, and that Shen Xiaoming would no longer serve as Secretary, Standing Committee member or member of the Hunan Provincial Party Committee. That is the checkable event fact in the source.

Public reports did not provide a transaction counterparty, amount or event date, and they did not describe internal working arrangements of the Hunan Provincial Party Committee. A sales analysis must not fill those blanks with imagined customer behavior. It must not turn a personnel adjustment directly into a conclusion that a particular supplier has an opportunity or a risk.

File Hunan government, state-owned enterprise, and industrial-park changes as signals; verify entry points, approval rhythm, evaluation criteria, and behavior evidence; keep blanks without proof.

| Item | What the source establishes | What a sales team should do |
| Leadership appointment | Publicly reported decision | Record the fact and its public source |
| Internal work arrangements | Not described | Do not infer; ask process questions |
| Counterparty, amount, event date | Not provided | Leave blank and seek evidence |
| Supplier opportunity or risk | Not established | Treat as a hypothesis until behavior confirms it |

## Relationship Capability: Find the Arm, Then Map the Decision Chain

Relationship capability is about building customer relationships through professional competence, decision information and trustworthy action. The emphasis is on understanding and covering the customer's decision chain, not on equating relationships with entertainment. After a leadership adjustment, whether an old contact remains effective cannot be settled by feeling. It needs one verifiable conversation.

PRTABCD review: past information, needs, time, Arm (who embraces you), budget, competitors, decision chain; record knowns, unknowns, sources, next questions, prioritizing A and D after leadership changes.

Ask who initiated recent communication, provides written material, or arranges cross-department meetings; for D, record participation order and each node's stop/continue authority.

### A Verifiable Conversation Beats an Old Contact

Downgrade old relationships when contacts cannot name the new decision chain, timelines, evaluation criteria; verify answers, mark silence unverified, avoid assuming longevity or quick endorsement.

## Questioning Capability: Ask for Process Facts, Not Personal Opinions

Questioning capability means designing questions around background, difficulty, impact and need payoff so that customers can see the problem and the value of change for themselves. The four SPIN question types can be used as follows: situation or background questions confirm facts; problem or difficulty questions locate blockers; implication questions make the cost visible; and need-payoff questions let the customer describe the benefit of change.

After a leadership adjustment, start with process facts. Through what channel is the new leader's arrival information confirmed? Have the approval sequence for project initiation, budget and procurement changed in a publicly checkable way? Which evaluation criteria remain unchanged? Which milestones need to be reconfirmed? These questions do not ask the contact to evaluate an individual. They ask the contact to describe process and evidence.

Do not ask, 'The leader has changed, so does that affect us?' That puts the contact in a difficult position. Replace it with three specific questions: Who will attend the next review? Who signs the materials? Who confirms the timeline? The answers are easier to check, and the questions respect the contact's boundaries. The goal of questioning is recordable information, not forcing a statement of loyalty.

## System Capability: Use Stage Evidence to Decide Whether to Reset

Dual-view capability funnel matches five sales stages—contact, needs, solution, negotiation, close—to seven procurement stages from discovery to commitment/signing, using evidence and continuous action.

After a leadership change, a project may slip from the solution stage back to the needs stage, or it may stall at the point where responsibility ownership is confirmed. The basis for judgment is customer behavior: Has someone claimed the goal? Has the budget source been confirmed? Have evaluation criteria been provided? Has a formal commitment been formed? Visit counts are only activity records.

If the customer has not confirmed ownership of responsibility and the sales team rushes to quote, that is a stage mismatch. If the customer has already entered solution and supplier selection while the sales team is still repeating background introductions, the action is behind the stage. Put the project into the dual-view funnel and check each item.

## Value Capability: Turn Change Into a Customer Account

Value capability frames offerings as profit, investment, business value; during leadership change, a PIP covers protected/increased profit, confidence, mechanism, timeframe—investment case, not price list.

If the customer has not provided a budget and evaluation criteria, do not use discounts to buy progress. A discount narrows the room for later negotiation and pulls the discussion back to price. A more stable approach is to write input, return, payback period and risk into one checkable account, and then discuss payment milestones and scope.

## Negotiation Capability: Protect the Bottom Line Before Trading Conditions

Negotiation capability is the ability to design concessions and conditional exchanges on the basis of clear power, time, information, bottom line and alternatives, so as to reach a sustainable agreement. BATNA is the best alternative to a negotiated agreement: the best fallback action a negotiator can take when the parties cannot reach agreement. In preparation, specify the executable alternative, bottom line, authority, interests and record.

A leadership adjustment does not mean a contract is automatically renegotiated, but sales teams should prepare conditional exchanges: price for payment milestones, scope for acceptance criteria, and time for resource commitment. A concession without a condition only makes the next negotiation harder. Before negotiating, write down the bottom line, what can be traded and what cannot. That is more reliable than making statements in the room.

## A Practical Checklist for the Next Five Working Days

- [ ] Record the public appointment fact and its source, and keep it separate from hypotheses.
- [ ] Run a PRTABCD gap review and mark known, unknown, source and next question for all seven items.
- [ ] Hold one verifiable conversation to confirm whether A and D have changed.
- [ ] Mark the project once on the five sales stages and once on the seven procurement stages; note any mismatch.
- [ ] Draft a one-page PIP with profit impact, confidence, mechanism, timeframe, payback and risk.
- [ ] Write the BATNA, bottom line, authority, tradable items and non-tradable items before the next negotiation.
- [ ] Review the account through all five PSS5 capabilities and assign one next action to each gap.

## FAQ

### Why should key-account sales teams care about the Hunan leadership adjustment?

It is an external change signal. Teams should check whether information entry, decision chain and project rhythm have changed. The checkable fact is the appointment decision reported by the public source. Public reports do not provide a counterparty, amount, date or internal arrangements. Do not fill those blanks with customer behavior.

### What can be written into an account analysis?

Publicly reported appointment information and the Hunan Provincial Party Committee as the subject. Do not write a counterparty, amount, event date, internal working arrangements or a supplier opportunity or risk conclusion. Separate verified fact from unverified assumption.

### What does relationship capability do first when a key person changes?

Review the decision chain with PRTABCD, focusing on A and D. Ask the old contact for names, timelines and evaluation criteria. If the contact cannot answer, mark the relationship as unverified.

### How can sales ask without offending the customer?

Ask process facts, not personal evaluations. Ask who attends the next review, who signs materials, who confirms the timeline, how arrival information is confirmed and whether the approval sequence has changed. Specific questions are easier to verify.

### How does system capability decide whether to reset?

Map the project on the sales and procurement stages, then use behavior evidence: goal ownership, budget source, evaluation criteria and formal commitment. Visit counts are only activity records.

### How should PSS5 be used together?

Relationship capability checks the decision chain; questioning capability gets process facts; system capability locates the stage; value capability writes the PIP; negotiation capability prepares the BATNA and conditional exchanges. Use the same public event, but do not describe advice as customer behavior that has already happened.
