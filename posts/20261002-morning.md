A publicly disclosed disposal agreement can look routine. For key-account sellers, it shows how a customer buys certainty. Guangdong Feinan Resources Utilization Co., Ltd. won a bid to handle destruction-type scrapped materials for a communications equipment enterprise, then signed a disposal agreement with a related entity. Expected volume is about 21,000 tonnes, calculated by winning share. The agreement runs from October 1, 2026, to September 30, 2028. Actual collection quantities and purchase amounts are subject to actual collection.

That last sentence matters: the contract is a volume framework plus per-transaction settlement, so revenue and cost both float with actual collection.

## What the Contract Structure Actually Tells You

Two features stand out. The term is long: two years of validity means continuous delivery, and renewal becomes the starting point of the next campaign rather than an afterthought. Settlement is uncertain: both sides accepted volume fluctuation at signing, which usually signals the customer is buying a certainty capability, not a one-time clearance.

This is where PSS5 value capability becomes a practical lens. Value capability means converting the sales object from product features into an investment, return, and profit improvement the customer can understand, and explaining that value with business numbers. Here the communications equipment enterprise is not simply buying disposal. It is likely managing compliance disposal, information security destruction, inventory repositioning, and site occupation. The seller won because it wrote several things clearly into the customer's ledger: I can absorb this volume, I have compliant disposal capability, and handing it to me is less trouble than organizing it internally.

### The Two Boxes Behind the Budget

Value capability is about helping Box 2, the operating manager layer that uses funds, make a business case to Box 1, the capital allocation and approval layer. In destruction and disposal work, Box 2 carries daily cost pressure. If the seller can explain how disposal reduces compliance risk exposure and frees up site and labor, Box 1's reason to approve shifts from spending money to preventing loss and releasing resources. That is the move from feature language to profit language.

To reuse this thinking in your own accounts, test yourself against three questions:

- Is the customer buying your service to save a specific cost, or to avoid a specific risk? Can you say it in one sentence?
- Can the value be written as an investment case: how much improvement, with what mechanism and confidence, over what time frame?
- Who inside the customer is accountable for this money? Can the materials you provide be taken directly by that person to the next level?

## The Two-Year Term Tests System and Relationship Capability

Signing is only the completion of the closing stage in the funnel. The real test is the delivery period. In PSS5 system capability terms, the sales funnel is fixed at five stages: contact, needs, solution, negotiation, and closing, with progress judged by customer-side exit evidence. Post-closing actions belong to delivery and account management, but delivery quality directly determines the starting point of the next funnel cycle. Over two years, every collection and every settlement statement is the customer scoring you through behavior evidence.

Relationship capability also changes dynamically during this phase. During the contract period, contacts, approval processes, and business pressures can all shift. PSS5 relationship capability emphasizes building customer relationships through professional ability, decision information, and credible action, focusing on understanding and covering the customer decision chain rather than equating relationships with socializing. A concrete method is the PRTABCD seven-item decision information scan:

- P: past and historical information
- R: needs
- T: timing and key milestones
- A: advocate, meaning who in the customer organization is embracing you
- B: budget
- C: competitors and alternatives
- D: decision chain, meaning the multi-person, multi-department decision process

For each item, record what is known, what is unknown, the information source, and the next question. Keep updating this table throughout the contract period. Before renewal negotiations, you will know which information gaps must be filled during the solution stage.

## Three Operational Judgments for Similar Key-Account Sellers

First, a volume framework plus per-transaction settlement shows the customer is willing to pay a premium for certainty. When quoting, price stable delivery capability rather than sliding into per-tonne price comparison.

Second, actual collection as the settlement basis means both sides must manage fluctuation. Whoever helps the customer establish a predictable collection schedule and settlement rhythm first gains an extra bargaining chip at the table.

Third, a two-year validity period is a natural review window. Customer behavior evidence from each delivery should flow back into funnel stage judgments, building facts for the next bid rather than relying on relationship impressions from the last round.

PSS5 places five dimensions, namely value capability, relationship capability, questioning capability, system capability, and negotiation capability, on the same operational map. The announcement does not show negotiation details, but it is reasonable to infer that signing a two-year agreement means the seller had no obvious weakness in its calculation of power, time, information, and alternatives. For anyone pursuing similar projects, the value capability homework can start today: write down the costs you can save, the risks you can avoid, and the resources you can free up, one by one, as business numbers the customer can understand.

## Actionable Checklist

- Write your value as an investment case, not a quotation: improvement, mechanism, confidence, and time frame.
- Identify Box 2, the operating manager who uses funds, and Box 1, the approval layer that allocates capital.
- Translate your service into at least one cost saving, one risk reduction, and one resource release.
- Build and maintain a PRTABCD table, recording known, unknown, source, and next question for each item.
- Judge funnel progress by customer-side exit evidence, not by visit count or self-assessment.
- After each delivery, capture customer behavior evidence and feed it back into the funnel stage judgment.
- Before renewal, review which information gaps remain and fill them during the solution stage.
- Avoid per-unit price comparison when the customer is buying certainty; price the capability instead.

In cost-type businesses such as destruction and disposal, Box 2 carries daily disposal cost and compliance pressure, but spending requires Box 1 approval. The seller's value capability move is to help Box 2 write the disposal service as business value, so that Box 1's approval reason shifts from spending money to preventing loss and releasing resources.
