An industrial equipment announcement can open a useful customer conversation. Its commercial meaning still depends on the customer's operating conditions, decision process and alternatives. A reported increase in domestic component sourcing, for example, raises questions about supply, maintenance and purchasing terms; it does not establish a particular customer's financial return.

This guide uses a reported lithography platform milestone to show how key-account teams can turn public information into practical account work. The approach combines five capabilities: value, relationships, questioning, process management and negotiation. Use them together as the opportunity requires.

## Establish the facts and their limits

The supplied source cites an Eastmoney report about Shanghai Micro Electronics Equipment Group. According to that account, its independently developed, next-generation general technology platform for front-end dry lithography equipment appeared at the ninth China International Import Expo. Domestic sourcing exceeded 90% of key components, and the platform had reached volume production.

The report also described multiple machines operating stably over an extended period on leading domestic logic and memory production lines, with cumulative wafer processing exceeding ten million wafers.

Those statements define the factual starting point. The supplied account does not disclose transaction counterparties, contract values, signing dates, specific customer identities or internal purchasing arrangements. Do not infer them. Nor does the reported operating history establish performance under every prospective customer's conditions.

Keep three categories separate in account discussions: reported facts, customer-specific evidence and questions still awaiting answers.

## Translate technical progress into operating value

A component localization percentage becomes commercially useful when connected to the customer's operations. Start with three areas:

- **Replenishment:** What delivery commitments and inventory responsibilities could the customer reasonably request?
- **Downtime recovery:** What operating records would help assess stability and recovery expectations?
- **Lifecycle economics:** How do the purchase, service and spare-parts arrangements affect the investment case?

Higher domestic sourcing provides a reason to investigate supply control. It does not, by itself, prove shorter lead times or lower costs. Likewise, the reported production record provides evidence to examine, rather than a substitute for customer verification.

### Build a profit improvement proposal

A PIP, or profit improvement proposal, should answer three questions: how much profit could be increased or protected, what mechanism and evidence support that estimate, and over what period the benefit could occur.

Place the initial purchase and subsequent service obligations in the same investment assessment. Identify the operating activity affected before assigning financial value. If the customer's downtime costs or maintenance records are unavailable, leave the estimate open and specify the data needed. A blank field is more useful than an unsupported saving.

## Map relationships through decision information

Relationship capability means using professional understanding, decision information and credible action to cover the buying organization. PRTABCD provides seven categories for identifying information gaps:

| Category | Account question |
| --- | --- |
| P: Past information | What relevant history shapes this purchase? |
| R: Requirements | What does the customer need to change? |
| T: Timing | Which milestones govern the decision? |
| A: Arm | Who supports the proposal internally, and why? |
| B: Budget | What is known about funding? |
| C: Competition and alternatives | What other courses of action remain available? |
| D: Decision | How does the cross-functional decision chain work? |

A specifically means Arm: the internal supporter willing to speak for the proposal with evidence. D means Decision: the chain involving multiple people and departments, rather than a single senior executive.

For production equipment, investigate A and D carefully. Which process or equipment owner would explain the operating evidence? Who carries switching risk? Who can veto, sign and accept delivery? Also examine alternatives such as maintaining existing equipment or building internal repair capability. Record knowns, unknowns, sources and the next question for each category.

## Use questions to uncover business consequences

SPIN structures questioning around situation, problem, implication and need-payoff. Begin by establishing operating facts, then help the customer explain their significance.

1. **Situation:** What is the current replenishment cycle for critical components, and who tracks it?
2. **Problem:** Which type of downtime has been hardest to resolve during the past year?
3. **Implication:** How does that downtime affect shipment plans and external commitments, and where is the loss recorded?
4. **Need-payoff:** If replenishment became faster, which operating measure would improve first?

These questions connect equipment characteristics to the customer's reasons for change. Follow the customer's answers rather than supplying the conclusion. The purpose is to establish what should change, why it matters and when action is warranted.

## Match sales activity to purchasing progress

A dual-perspective funnel tracks the seller's work alongside the buyer's decision tasks.

The five sales stages are contact, needs, solution, negotiation and closing. The seven purchasing stages are identifying a problem, assessing value, confirming that a solution exists, assigning responsibility, deciding whether to build internally or buy externally, selecting a solution and supplier, and commitment and signing.

These stages have different levels of detail and a many-to-many relationship. They should not be forced into a one-to-one sequence.

Consider a customer who recognizes a component supply problem and accepts that domestic equipment could address it, but has not assigned responsibility for switching risk. Sending a quotation does not resolve that unfinished decision task. The useful next step is to clarify ownership and success criteria.

Assess progress through customer behavior: relevant people attend meetings, operating data is supplied, or the issue enters a budget discussion. Visit counts and the seller's confidence do not establish purchasing progress. Review whether seller activity is aligned with, ahead of or behind the customer's work.

## Prepare alternatives before negotiating terms

BATNA means the best alternative to a negotiated agreement: the best executable course of action available if agreement cannot be reached.

Before negotiation, document the alternative action, minimum acceptable position, authority limits and underlying interests. Keep a record of the preparation and subsequent exchanges. Consider the other party's alternatives without treating assumptions about them as established facts.

A buyer might continue operating existing equipment or develop internal repair capacity. A supplier might pursue another production line or customer. These are possible alternatives to investigate, not disclosed arrangements in the reported case.

With alternatives understood, negotiate exchanges across delivery schedules, acceptance criteria, spare-parts inventory and service response. Connect a concession to a corresponding condition. This makes the discussion about the agreement's operating terms as well as its price.

## An actionable account checklist

Use the following checklist to turn the announcement into a specific next step:

- Prepare a shared, one-page fact sheet covering the reported sourcing percentage, volume production and operating record. Record the report's provenance and attach its source link when available.
- Mark undisclosed customer identities, contract values and purchasing arrangements as unknown.
- Identify which supply, downtime or lifecycle-cost question matters to the account.
- Request the customer data needed for a profit improvement proposal; leave unsupported estimates blank.
- Update all seven PRTABCD categories, prioritizing the internal supporter and decision chain.
- Use SPIN questions to establish operating consequences and desired improvements.
- Mark the customer's purchasing stage using observable behavior, then choose a matching action.
- Document negotiation alternatives and the conditions available for exchange.

Managers can divide decision-chain coverage among technical, service and commercial colleagues. Review the evidence together so that the team works from a consistent account picture.

## FAQ

### Does a domestic sourcing rate above 90% prove customer savings?

No. It identifies a reason to examine replenishment, inventory responsibility, recovery time and service terms. Savings require customer operating data and a supported mechanism connecting the proposed change to financial value.

### Can the reported production record establish suitability for a particular line?

It provides operating evidence worth checking. The supplied account does not establish suitability for every line or disclose specific customers. Keep the reported record distinct from the prospective customer's acceptance requirements.

### Must all five capabilities be used in order?

No. They can operate together. A question about downtime may reveal both a value issue and a missing decision participant; negotiation preparation may uncover an alternative that changes the account assessment.

### What should happen when the buyer accepts the value but ownership is unclear?

Clarify who carries responsibility for the change and what success means. Match the next sales action to that unresolved purchasing task. A quotation alone supplies neither ownership nor acceptance criteria.

### What should a team do when important evidence is missing?

Record the gap, its information source and the next question. Keep financial estimates open until customer data supports them, and distinguish possible alternatives from confirmed purchasing plans.
