## Why a Two-Sentence Recommendation Is Worth Studying

A securities research note recommended a major fiberglass company for exactly two reasons: electronic cloth prices were continuing to rise, which was expected to lift earnings, and a subsidiary planned to invest 4.431 billion yuan to build a production line with annual capacity of 50,000 tons of electronic yarn and 320 million meters of electronic cloth. No adjectives, no narrative, only a price fact and a capacity commitment. That is what a competent value statement looks like.

The structure of that recommendation is the same structure a key-account salesperson needs when writing a value case for a customer. This guide reads the note as a working lesson in translating product features into investment, return, and profit improvement language, and shows how to apply the same discipline to enterprise selling.

## The Standard Structure Hiding in Institutional Recommendation Language

The recommendation has two layers: a market fact (electronic cloth prices continuing to rise) and a company action (the subsidiary plans to invest 4.431 billion yuan in a line producing 50,000 tons of electronic yarn and 320 million meters of electronic cloth annually). Stacked together, the reader draws the conclusion without being told: rising prices mean existing lines earn more, expansion amplifies the improvement, so earnings are expected to grow. Everything rests on verifiable facts and numbers.

PSS5 value capability is defined as converting the object of a sale from product function into an investment, return, and profit improvement the customer can understand, and explaining that value with operating numbers. Measured against that definition, the recommendation completes three translations. First, it states the phenomenon (price increase) alongside the action (4.431 billion yuan, 50,000 tons, 320 million meters) rather than describing either in isolation. Second, the numbers imply a causal link instead of asserting a vague outlook. Third, the conclusion is left for the reader to reach, which raises credibility rather than lowering it.

Most B2B value propositions stall at the first step because the seller opens with product parameters instead of the money on the customer's books.

### Keep the Boundary Between Forecast and Guarantee

The research note says earnings are expected to grow and that expansion is expected to drive continued improvement. That is predictive language, and it is a research opinion, not a reported result. When sales teams borrow this style, they should keep the same restraint: write the improvement your solution creates for the customer's operations as an investment case, not as a personal guarantee.

## Four Moves Key-Account Sales Can Borrow

Translating the way an analyst writes a recommendation into key-account selling produces four concrete steps, each mapping to a practiceable action within PSS5 value capability.

1. List the changes actually happening on the customer's books. As with the electronic cloth price increase, find price, cost, or demand shifts in the customer's industry that have already occurred and that the customer acknowledges. Do not fabricate and do not exaggerate.

2. Convert your solution into capacity and return language. The 4.431 billion yuan investment was not written as advanced, intelligent, or leading. It was written as 50,000 tons of electronic yarn and 320 million meters of electronic cloth per year. Sales should likewise convert features into outputs the customer can check.

3. State improvement in a predictive voice, not a guaranteeing voice. Expected to drive earnings growth is a bounded expression. A profit improvement proposal should state the mechanism and the level of confidence so the buyer sees the basis rather than hearing a slogan.

4. Leave the numbers for the customer to verify. Give the annual capacity figure and the customer will calculate how much demand it covers. Give the price reduction and the customer will calculate the cost saved. Verifiable numbers are what build trust.

Once these four moves become habit, what you hand a customer is no longer a product introduction. It is a return statement that circulates internally without needing translation.

## Why Most Value Statements Fail Internal Circulation

Three failure patterns recur.

The first is all adjectives. Stable, efficient, industry-leading fill the page, and the customer cannot use any of it to explain to a budget approver why the money is worth spending.

The second is all self-referential parameters. Technical specifications are listed one after another, but nothing answers which cost line or revenue line they affect for the customer.

The third is over-promising. A forecast is written as a guarantee, and when delivery fluctuates, relationship capital and negotiating position are damaged at the same time.

Against the two-sentence recommendation, the gap is obvious: it presents a combination of facts the customer can verify from their own vantage point. To build the same habit, ask three questions before writing anything. Which line on the customer's books will change because of my solution? What number expresses the size of that change? How can the customer verify that number? If all three can be answered, the value statement stands.

| Failure pattern | What it looks like | What the customer cannot do |
|---|---|---|
| All adjectives | Stable, efficient, leading | Explain the spend to an approver |
| Parameters only | A list of technical specs | Connect specs to a cost or revenue line |
| Over-promising | Forecast written as guarantee | Trust the claim after any delivery variance |

## A Practical Exercise You Can Run This Week

Pick one priority account you are working on this week. Imitating the structure of a brokerage recommendation, write two reasons for your proposal: one change that has already occurred in the customer's industry, and one specific input and output your proposal corresponds to. Then hand it to a colleague who does not understand your product and ask them to read it.

If they can restate what the customer will gain, the statement passes. If they can only restate what your product does, go back to step one and translate again. Value capability is not a talent. It is a writing and questioning motion that can be practiced repeatedly.

## Actionable Checklist

- Identify one verifiable change already occurring in the customer's industry or cost structure.
- Write it as a fact, not as an adjective or a slogan.
- Convert each product feature into a capacity, cost, or revenue figure the customer can check.
- State the improvement as a forecast with a named mechanism, not as a guarantee.
- Ask which line on the customer's books changes, by how much, and how it can be verified.
- Run an internal blind read with a colleague unfamiliar with the product.
- Confirm the reader restates customer gains, not product functions.
- Separate facts you can source from opinions and predictions you are relaying.
- Avoid conclusions the source material does not support.

## FAQ

### What problem does PSS5 value capability actually solve?

It bridges what a salesperson says and what appears on the customer's books, converting the object of a sale from product function into an investment, return, and profit improvement the customer can understand through operating numbers. Many key-account deals are lost at the proposal stage because the conversation stays on features the customer cannot use to justify a budget internally.

### What does a securities recommendation have to do with selling?

The connection is the structure of the expression. The recommendation offered two lines: rising electronic cloth prices were expected to lift earnings, and the subsidiary planned to invest 4.431 billion yuan in a line producing 50,000 tons of electronic yarn and 320 million meters of electronic cloth annually. No adjectives, only verifiable facts and numbers, so the reader reaches the conclusion independently. Customer-facing value statements often err by piling on adjectives and reciting parameters.

### What if my numbers are not precise?

Distinguish two kinds of imprecision. When the source itself is a forecast, as when the research note says earnings are expected to grow, bounded predictive language is appropriate: state the mechanism and confidence level without guaranteeing an outcome. When the numbers belong to your own proposal, define them jointly with the customer: which steps are affected, what measurement basis applies, and who verifies. A conservative estimate both sides accept beats an impressive figure that cannot be reconciled.

### How does value capability relate to a profit growth proposal?

A profit growth proposal is the core vehicle through which value capability lands. It writes the improvement to the customer's operations as an investment case and answers three questions: how much profit is added or protected, what the mechanism and confidence level are, and over what time frame. It is not a price quote. A quote answers what the customer spends; a proposal answers why the spend is worthwhile.

### The customer says our solution looks similar to a competitor. How does value capability break the tie?

When products are commoditized, differentiation can only appear at the translation layer.

### What are the boundaries when citing external reports or industry information?

Three lines. First, cite only information that exists and that you can source. The 4.431 billion yuan investment and the 50,000 tons of electronic yarn and 320 million meters of electronic cloth figures come directly from the report and can be checked. Second, separate fact from opinion: the analyst's judgment is a research opinion, not an accomplished result. Third, avoid overreach; the report says earnings are expected to grow, which does not support claims that orders are full or operations are solid.

### How do I verify a value statement once it is written?

A simple test is an internal blind read. Give the statement to a colleague unfamiliar with your product and ask them to restate what the customer gains. If they restate returns and numbers, the translation worked. If they restate product functions, the statement is still at the parameter layer. A stricter test is to place the material with the customer and see whether a key stakeholder can relay it to their superior without you in the room.
