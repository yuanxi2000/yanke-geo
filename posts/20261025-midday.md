## Start with the fact boundary

## Value capability: translate the project topic into business language

Value capability translates a product or service into customer profit, investment and operating value. For a membrane materials and membrane module capability improvement project, start with why the customer would discuss capability building now, not with product parameters. The project name shows a direction; benefit, priority and acceptance criteria still have to come from the customer.

Prepare three operating questions:

- Which operating result should the capability improvement change first: delivery stability, quality performance or new-product introduction speed?
- If the project is delayed, which business metric is affected first, and how does the customer measure it?
- What criterion makes the investment worthwhile inside the customer organization, and who owns that criterion?

These questions have no standard answers. The seller’s job is to record the customer’s answers and restate value in the customer’s language. If the customer cannot explain the benefit, ask more questions; do not invent a polished benefits table on the customer’s behalf.

## Relationship capability: use PRTABCD to expose intelligence gaps

Relationship capability builds customer relationships through professional capability, decision information and credible action, with emphasis on understanding and covering the decision chain. A public mention only creates a topic entry. It does not prove who supports the project, who owns the budget or who influences selection. Use a scan table to list unknowns.

PRTABCD’s seven information fields can be used as follows:

- Fill known fields first: subject, public source and project name.
- Mark unknown fields: who initiates, who uses, who evaluates and who approves.
- For each unknown, write one next question and one possible source of information.
- Do not turn guesses into facts. A stands for Arm, the person or group in the customer organization that is embracing you.

A senior name is not completion. Progress means you can verify what the person cares about and what the person can influence. If the project enters your account list, the first conversation should aim to find someone willing to add context and to earn a next business discussion.

## Questioning capability: use SPIN so the customer states the case for change

Questioning capability discovers and amplifies real demand through intentional question design. SPIN separates questions into Situation, Problem, Implication and Need-payoff layers.

Use questions such as:

- Situation: Which part of delivery, quality or new-product introduction is most often questioned by business units today?
- Problem: Which orders or projects have had to be adjusted recently because capability was insufficient?
- Implication: If the issue continues into the next budget cycle, which revenue, cost or customer commitment is affected?
- Need-payoff: After capability improvement, which business result does the customer want to see first?

The purpose is to let the customer complete the value calculation. Do not ask all four layers in one call, and do not jump to a solution after the first pain point. The more specific the answers, the firmer the later value explanation and solution boundary.

## System capability: news heat is not funnel evidence

System capability uses a dual-view funnel made of the sales five stages and the customer’s seven purchasing stages. A public mention at most creates a contact topic. It does not prove that the customer has confirmed a need or entered a solution stage. Check evidence stage by stage:

- Has the customer voluntarily added project background and goals?
- Will the customer arrange a business owner for the next discussion?
- Has the customer given evaluation criteria, timing or budget language?

If any answer is blank, do not advance the stage. Writing news heat as sales progress distorts later resource decisions and commitments.

## Negotiation capability: prepare conditions before price

Negotiation capability designs concessions and condition exchanges after clarifying power, time, information, bottom line and alternatives, with the aim of a sustainable agreement. Record the customer’s requirements on scope, delivery, acceptance, payment and risk sharing early. Separate what can be given from what must be exchanged.

If the topic reaches quotation, do not treat price as the only variable. Confirm whether the customer is buying capability, delivery certainty or risk reduction. Write service scope, acceptance method, payment rhythm and risk responsibility as exchangeable conditions. If no alternative exists, gather information and prepare backup actions before formal negotiation. Keep records of customer language, condition changes, approvers and the next confirmation time. Clear records keep concessions bounded and agreements executable.

## From signal to action: a five-step sequence

Turn one public item into sales action in five steps:

1. Record facts only: Wote Technology, the public source and the membrane materials and membrane module capability improvement project. Leave missing fields blank.
2. Prepare four SPIN questions, one for each layer.
3. Complete the PRTABCD seven-field scan with known, unknown, source and next question.
4. Check the sales five-stage funnel against customer behavior evidence.
5. Prepare negotiation conditions for scope, delivery, acceptance, payment and risk, plus alternatives.

Q4: What is the common SPIN mistake?
